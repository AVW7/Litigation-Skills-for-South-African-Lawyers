# Litigation Skills for South African Lawyers
## Digital Vault Dashboard

Welcome to the digital, interactive vault for Chris Marnewick SC's *Litigation Skills for South African Lawyers*. This vault transforms the 319 pages of the text into a fully linked knowledge base.

> [!important] Court Protocol Mode of Address
> - **High Court / SCA / Constitutional Court:** Address the judge as **"My Lord"**, **"My Lady"**, **"M'Lord"**, or **"M'Lady"** (Third person: *"Your Lordship"* or *"Her Ladyship"*). Never say "you" or "yourself".
> - **Magistrates' Court:** Address the magistrate as **"Your Worship"**.
> - **In Chambers:** Address the judge as **"Judge"**.
> - **Opposing Counsel:** Address exclusively as **"my learned friend"** or **"my learned friends"**.
> - **Witnesses:** Formal address only (**"Mr"**, **"Mrs"**, **"Ms"**, or by rank like **"Doctor"**, **"Sergeant"**).

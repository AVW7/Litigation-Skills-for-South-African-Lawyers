### 1.5 Statement of Simon Reddy

I am a constable in the South African Police Service.

On 12 December [year] I was on duty on patrol in a police van in [name of street] when I received a radio message at about 10:30 to go to Three Rings Sports to follow up a complaint of shoplifting.

I arrived at Three Rings Sport about 10 minutes later. The accused was handed over to me by Irene Delamere, together with two backpacks. The one was red, brand new, and had a price tag with the store's log and the price on it. The price was marked as R150.00. The other backpack was a rusty brown colour and was old and torn. It had an old newspaper in it. Ms Delamere told me what had happened in the presence of the accused. She said that the accused had taken the store's backpack without paying for it. His own backpack was later found on the floor next to the rack where the backpacks were on display. The accused said nothing. I asked Ms Delamere to come to the police station after 2pm so that I could take a statement from her.

I warned the accused in terms of the Judge's Rules and he elected not to say anything. He asked if he could telephone his mother. I told him he could do that from the police station. I then arrested the accused and informed him of the charge against him.

At the police station I searched the accused before placing him in the cells. I found R45.00 in banknotes in his trousers pocket. He had no other money on him.

I entered the two backpacks in the SAP 13 register and opened a docket.
I warned the accused in terms of the Judges Rules and after he had indicated that he understood his rights, he elected not to make a statement.

Later the same day I took a statement from Ms Delamere, entered in the docket as 'A1'.

*S Reddy*

Constable no 1325475

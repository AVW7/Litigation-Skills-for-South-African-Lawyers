## 🧠 Key Concepts

**Facta Probanda** = Material facts you *must prove* (plead these)

**Facta Probantia** = Evidence that proves facts (do NOT plead)

**The Cross-Exam Duty** = Put your version to the witness on contradicted points

**The Prayer** = Every pleading needs clear relief sought

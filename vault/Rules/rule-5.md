# Citations of Legal Authorities

All authorities must be cited clearly, consistently, and accurately in South African practice. (See [[Chapter 13 - Preparation for trial Legal research|Chapter 13]])

---

### 📜 Statutes
Refer to the statute by its full name and number on the first occasion (e.g., *the Prescription Act 68 of 1969*), and by a shortened title (e.g., *the Prescription Act* or *Act 68 of 1969*) subsequently.

### ⚖️ Case Law
Cite cases using their standard South African law report references (e.g., *S v Makwanyane 1995 (3) SA 391 (CC)*). Specify the page and paragraph when citing a particular passage.
* **Format:** `[Parties] [Year] ([Volume]) [Report] [Page] ([Court])`
  * Example: *S v Makwanyane 1995 (3) SA 391 (CC)*
  * Example: *R v Dhlumayo 1948 (2) SA 677 (A)*

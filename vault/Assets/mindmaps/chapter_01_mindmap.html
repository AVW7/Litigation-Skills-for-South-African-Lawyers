👥 Chapter 1: Interviewing Clients & Witnesses

South African Litigation Skills - Interactive Study Mind Map

📄
8-Stage Framework
• 1. Meeting & Pleasantries
Meet in reception, no titles
• 2. Problem & Goal ID
Listen first, no note-taking
• 3. Preliminary Matters
Fees, privilege, conflicts
• 4. Chronological Facts
Funnelling: open to closed
• 5. Case Theory
Facta probanda vs probantia
• 6. Preliminary Advice
Be conservative
• 7. Concluding
Get authority, confirm letter
• 8. After Interview
Write up immediately
📄

# 📝 Practice Questions & Self-Assessment

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Use these questions to test your understanding of each lecture's key concepts. Questions are organized by section and include both recall questions and application scenarios.

---

## Section 1: Pre-Litigation (L00-L04)

### L00 - Introduction & Prefaces

**Recall Questions:**
1. What is the primary purpose of studying litigation skills according to Marnewick?
2. List four core competencies that a litigation practitioner must develop.
3. What is the relationship between theory and practice in legal education?

**Application Scenario:**
A newly admitted attorney asks you for advice on how to develop their litigation skills. Drawing on the themes in the prefaces, outline a 5-year professional development plan for them.

---

### L01 - Interviewing Clients and Witnesses

**Recall Questions:**
1. List the 7 stages of the client interview structure.
2. What is the difference between open, closed, and leading questions?
3. Define "funnelling" as a questioning technique.
4. What are the safeguards for interviewing witnesses?

**Application Scenario:**
Mrs Nkosi comes to your office after a motor vehicle accident. Her husband was killed, and she has three minor children. She needs immediate financial assistance.
- (a) Conduct the interview using the 7-stage structure.
- (b) Identify at least 5 potential causes of action.
- (c) Apply the proof-making model to her loss of support claim.

**Practical Exercise:**
Draft a witness statement from your interview notes following the safeguard protocols mentioned in the chapter.

---

### L02 - Advising and Counselling Clients

**Recall Questions:**
1. What are the essential elements of a letter of advice?
2. How should you communicate uncertainty to a client?
3. What ethical obligations apply when giving advice?

**Application Scenario:**
Your client wants to know their chances of success in a defamation claim against a newspaper. Explain how you would structure your advice, including dealing with uncertainties and alternative options.

---

### L03 - Alternatives to Litigation

**Recall Questions:**
1. Compare and contrast negotiation, mediation, and arbitration.
2. When is arbitration mandatory in South Africa?
3. What are the advantages of ADR over litigation?

**Application Scenario:**
Two business partners in a close corporation have a dispute over the management of the business. They want to avoid litigation. Advise them on the most appropriate ADR method and explain the process.

---

### L04 - Preparing to Commence Action

**Recall Questions:**
1. What is the purpose of a letter of demand?
2. How does a letter of demand affect prescription?
3. List the documents you would need before issuing summons in a road accident claim.

**Application Scenario:**
Your client was injured in a slip-and-fall at a supermarket. Draft a letter of demand to the supermarket's insurer, including all necessary legal elements.

## Section 2: Pleadings (L05-L09)

### L05 - Function, Form and Style of Pleadings

**Recall Questions:**
1. Define and distinguish between *facta probanda* and *facta probantia*.
2. What is the "material fact" rule?
3. What are the consequences of over-pleading?

**Application Scenario:**
A junior colleague asks you to review their draft statement of claim. They have included:
- "The defendant was driving negligently"
- "Witness X will testify that she saw the collision"
- "The police report attached hereto proves the speed"

Identify which statements violate pleading principles and explain why.

---

### L06 - Drafting Statements of Claim

**Recall Questions:**
1. What must be included in a particulars of claim?
2. Explain the difference between a material fact and evidence.
3. How do you plead damages?

**Practical Exercise:**
Draft a complete particulars of claim for a delictual claim for damages arising from a motor vehicle collision where:
- Plaintiff: Mrs van der Merwe (pedestrian)
- Defendant: Mr Smith (driver)
- Date: 15 March 2024
- Place: Main Street, Pretoria
- Injuries: Broken leg, lost wages for 3 months, medical expenses R150,000
- Causation: Defendant ran a red light

## Mixed Scenario Questions

### Scenario 1: Full Litigation Process

Your client, a small business owner, has not been paid R500,000 for goods delivered to a corporate client. The debt is 18 months old.

1. Draft a letter of demand.
2. Draft a statement of claim (simplified).
3. Anticipate the defendant's plea.
4. Outline your trial preparation strategy.
5. Prepare an examination-in-chief question list for your client.
6. If judgment is given against you, advise on appeal prospects.

### Scenario 2: Personal Injury Claim

Your client was injured when she slipped on a wet floor at a shopping mall. There were no warning signs.

1. Conduct an intake interview (7-stage structure).
2. Identify the legal elements of her claim.
3. Apply the proof-making model.
4. Draft the material facts for her particulars of claim.
5. Prepare for cross-examination of the mall's safety officer.

## Answer Guidelines

> [!note] How to Use These Questions
> - Write full answers before checking the relevant chapter
> - Use the diagrams as visual aids
> - Practice articulating answers out loud (as you would in an exam)
> - Time yourself: aim for 25-30 minutes per application scenario

> [!tip] Marking Criteria
> Application scenarios are marked on:
> 1. **Accuracy** - Correct statement of legal principles (40%)
> 2. **Application** - Proper application to facts (30%)
> 3. **Structure** - Logical organization (20%)
> 4. **Completeness** - All relevant issues addressed (10%)

# Onus of Proof

## Formal definition

The legal obligation on a party to prove the allegations they make in a court case.

## In simple words

> [!tip] In simple words
> The duty to prove your story. If you can't prove it, you lose.
>
> **Analogy:** A scale. You must place enough weight on your side to tilt it.

## Why we need it

To determine which party bears the risk of failure if the evidence is insufficient to prove the case.

## PARAGRAPH-BY-PARAGRAPH RESPONSES

### Rule 22(2) Categories of Response:

For each paragraph in the Particulars of Claim, respond using one of four categories:

1. **Admissions** - Admit facts you know to be true
2. **Denials** - Deny facts you dispute (tactical or substantive)
3. **Non-admissions** - Neither admit nor deny due to lack of knowledge
4. **Avoidances** - Confess and avoid (e.g., payment, waiver, fraud)

---

## TEMPLATE RESPONSES

### 1. ADMISSIONS

**When to use:** Admit facts that are true and cannot prejudice your case.

**Template:**
```
AD PARAGRAPH [X]

[X]. The allegations contained in paragraph [X] of the particulars of claim are admitted.
```

**Example (based on Essberger precedent):**
```
AD PARAGRAPH 2

2. These allegations are admitted.
```

**Caution:** Rule 22(3) states that any allegation not denied or stated to be not admitted is deemed admitted. Be careful not to admit in error.

---

### 2. DENIALS

**Standard Denial (Golden Rule: "If in doubt, deny"):**

```
AD PARAGRAPH [X]

[X]. The defendant denies each and every allegation contained in paragraph [X] of the particulars of claim as if specifically traversed.
```

**Non-Admission Denial (no knowledge):**

```
AD PARAGRAPH [X]

[X]. The defendant has no knowledge as to the allegations contained in paragraph [X] and accordingly denies them.
```

**Admitting and Denying Technique (when plaintiff's allegation is too broad):**

```
AD PARAGRAPH [X]

[X]. As [specific capacity, e.g., owner of the property], the Defendant [admitted fact].

[X]. Save as aforesaid, the defendant denies each and every allegation herein contained as if specifically traversed.
```

**Example from Essberger:**
```
AD PARAGRAPH 3 TO 6

3. These allegations are denied.

Alternatively

4. If it was held that the statements complained of were made by the defendant [to the plaintiff as alleged], and [constitute breach/error as alleged], then the defendant denies, on the following grounds, that [liability element] was wrongful/unlawful:

4.1. [Specific defence ground 1]
4.2. [Specific defence ground 2 - in the alternative]
4.3. [Specific defence ground 3 - further alternatively]
```

---

### 3. NON-ADMISSIONS

**When to use:** When the Defendant genuinely has no knowledge of the facts alleged and wishes to put the Plaintiff to proof.

**Template:**
```
AD PARAGRAPH [X]

[X]. The defendant has no knowledge of the allegations contained in paragraph [X] of the particulars of claim and consequently neither admits nor denies these allegations and puts the plaintiff to the proof thereof.
```

**Key effect:** With a non-admission, the Plaintiff need not anticipate contradictory evidence from the Defendant - testing occurs through cross-examination only.

---

### 4. CONFESSION AND AVOIDANCE (Avoidances)

**Common avoidances:** Fraud, waiver, payment, release, accord and satisfaction, prescription, estoppel, res judicata, contributory negligence, set-off, octroi de jure.

**Template:**
```
AD PARAGRAPH [X]

[X]. The defendant confesses and avoids the allegations contained in paragraph [X] on the following grounds:

[X].1 [State when, where, and how the avoidance operates - all material facts must be pleaded]

[X].2 [Continue with specific circumstances]

[X].3 [Conclude how this defeats the Plaintiff's claim]
```

**Example - Payment:**
```
AD PARAGRAPH [Y] (THE CAUSE OF ACTION/PRAYER)

[Y]. The Defendant admits that the debt arose as alleged, but pleads that the plaintiff's claim has been satisfied and is extinguished by payment.

[Y].1 On or about [date], the Defendant paid the Plaintiff the sum of [amount] at [place, if relevant] in full and final settlement of the plaintiff's claim.

[Y].2 Payment was made by [method: cash / EFT / cheque no. XXX dated XXX].

[Y].3 The Plaintiff accepted such payment in full satisfaction of the claim.

[Y].4 In the premises, the Plaintiff is not entitled to the relief claimed.
```

**Example - Prescription:**
```
AD PARAGRAPH [Z]

[Z]. The defendant pleads that the plaintiff's claim is prescribed in terms of section 11(d) of the Prescription Act 68 of 1969, alternatively section 11(e) as the case may be.

[Z].1 The debt [or delictual claim] arose on [date] when [facts giving rise to the debt].

[Z].2 More than [three] years have elapsed since the date on which the debt became due.

[Z].3 The debt has not been acknowledged by the defendant.

[Z].4 The debt has not been interrupted by service of process.

[Z].5 In the premises, the plaintiff's claim has prescribed.
```

## PRAYER (CONCLUSION)

**Standard format:**

```
WHEREFORE the Defendant prays:

1. That the plaintiff's claim be dismissed;

2. That the plaintiff be ordered to pay the defendant's costs on [attorney and client / party and party] scale;

3. [If counterclaim included: That the defendant's counterclaim be granted with costs]

4. [Further and/or alternative relief.]
```

**Enhanced version with costs of two counsel (if applicable):**
```
WHEREFORE the Defendant prays that the Plaintiff's claim be dismissed with costs, including the costs of two counsel.
```

**Alternative prayers for defence:**

```
WHEREFORE the Defendant prays:

1. That the plaintiff's claim be dismissed;

2. Costs against the plaintiff on the attorney and client scale, inclusive of costs of two counsel;

3. [Include prayer for counterclaim if applicable]

4. Further and/or alternative relief.
```

## CRITICAL DRAFTING PRINCIPLES

### Golden Rules from De Rebus Article:

1. **"If in doubt, deny"** - Van Blerk SC (Advocate 2000 at 34)
2. Admissions made in error are difficult to withdraw - require explanation and no prejudice to plaintiff
3. Tactical denials are risky - only use if confident plaintiff cannot frustrate their purpose

### Facta Probanda vs Facta Probantia
- Plead only **facta probanda** (material facts to establish the defence)
- Exclude **facta probantia** (evidence by which those facts are proved)

### Line-by-Line Response
Rule 22 requires that you answer every allegation in the Plaintiff's particulars of claim. Each allegation must be:
- **Admitted**
- **Denied**
- **Confessed and avoided**
- **Non-admitted**

### Alternative Pleading
Defences may be pleaded in the alternative. Answers need not be consistent, provided they are specifically pleaded "in the alternative."

### Jurisdictional Defences
Exception: Exception must be raised within 10 days (Rule 23(1)(a)) afford plaintiff opportunity to cure within 15 days.

## COMMON DEFENCES BY TYPE OF ACTION

### Contractual Claims
- Payment
- Novation
- Set-off (if pleaded and claimed)
- Prescription (3 years from breach)
- Estoppel
- Failure to perform condition precedent
- Duress / Undue influence
- Misrepresentation
- Supervening impossibility

### Delictual Claims
- No wrongfulness
- No causation
- No fault
- Contributory negligence
- Consent/assumption of risk
- Prescription (3 years from act/quicquid)

### Defamation Claims
- Truth and public benefit
- Fair comment
- Privilege (absolute or qualified)
- Reasonable publication
- Apology and payment

## CHECKLIST BEFORE FILING

- [ ] Have you answered every paragraph of the particulars of claim?
- [ ] Have you admitted only facts you are certain about?
- [ ] Have you denied everything in doubt?
- [ ] Have you pleaded avoidances with full material facts (when, where, how)?
- [ ] Does your plea disclose a valid defence?
- [ ] Is the prayer clear and complete?
- [ ] Is the address for service valid (not a PO box)?
- [ ] Have you signed and dated?
- [ ] Do you need a counterclaim?
- [ ] Are you within filing time limits?

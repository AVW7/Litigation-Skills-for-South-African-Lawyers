# Cause of Action

## Formal definition

A factual situation or set of facts (facta probanda) that gives a person a right to sue another in court and obtain legal relief.

## In simple words

> [!tip] In simple words
> A legally recognized reason to sue someone. If you don't have a valid cause of action, your case will be thrown out.
>
> **Analogy:** A ticket that allows you to enter a ride. Without the ticket, you cannot get on.

## Why we need it

Every statement of claim or summons must disclose a valid cause of action. If it fails to do so, the defendant can file an exception under Rule 23, ending the litigation prematurely.
